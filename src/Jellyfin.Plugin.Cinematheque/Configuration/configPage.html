Movements

Built-in movements update with the plugin. List here the movements you add, and built-ins you change under their own Id. A film belongs to a movement when its TMDB id is in TmdbIds, or when it matches every rule that is set: one of the Countries (ISO codes or names), between YearFrom and YearTo, by one of the Directors, with one of the Genres and one of the Tags. Names are compared without accents or punctuation.
