Cinematheque

Browse your films by director, actor, country and film movement. The tab in the web client needs the File Transformation plugin.

Movements

Built-in movements come with the plugin and improve with each release. Editing one keeps your version instead; hiding one removes it from the tab. A film belongs to a movement when its TMDB id is listed, or when it matches every rule that is filled in: one of the countries, within the years, by one of the directors, with one of the genres and one of the tags.

Import or export as JSON
Your added and edited movements. Export, edit, then import to replace them in the form; nothing is saved until you press Save.

Collections

Movements then also appear in the TV and mobile apps, which do not show the Cinematheque tab. Collections are named in the server's display language and follow the movement's rules after every library scan: films added to them by hand are removed. Cinematheque never deletes a collection; when a movement is hidden or this option is turned off, its collection stays as it is.
